Opening the hood on a G20: pull the release lever in the driver's footwell twice. The first pull unlatches, the second releases the safety catch. Lift the hood; gas struts hold it up. To close, lower it to about 50 cm and let it drop, then press on the front edge until it clicks on both sides.

How your diesel engine makes power

A diesel has no spark plugs. It squeezes air so hard that it gets hot enough to ignite the fuel by itself. Each of your four cylinders repeats the four strokes below about 12 times a second at 1,500 rpm.

The supporting systems

SystemJobWhat you can check
LubricationAn oil pump pushes oil between all moving metal parts so they never touch. Oil also cools the turbocharger.Oil level on the screen, oil service on time.
CoolingA water and antifreeze mix carries heat from the engine to the radiator. A thermostat keeps the engine near 90 to 100 °C.Coolant level in the tank, engine cold.
ElectricalThe 12 V battery runs electronics and lights. On the mild hybrid, a 48 V starter-generator restarts the engine, recovers braking energy and gives a short boost.Slow or hesitant starting, battery messages.
BrakesThe pedal pressurises brake fluid, which clamps pads onto discs at each wheel. ABS and DSC modulate that pressure to keep the car steerable and stable.Fluid level, pad wear shown in the service menu.
Exhaust cleaningOxidation catalyst, particulate filter and AdBlue-fed SCR catalyst remove carbon monoxide, soot and nitrogen oxides.AdBlue range, regular longer drives.

Warning lights and what they are connected to

Colour tells you the urgency. Red: stop as soon as it is safe. Yellow: you can usually continue carefully, but act soon. Green or blue: information only. Your BMW also shows a text message (Check Control) with each light.

Your five-minute monthly check

Habits that keep a modern diesel healthy

  • Give it a long run. Many short city trips fill the particulate filter with soot. A 20 to 30 minute drive on a motorway or country road every week or two lets it burn clean.
  • Do not switch off mid-clean. If the cooling fan runs loudly after parking, idle is slightly higher, or you smell something hot, the filter was regenerating. No harm done, but try to finish the next one.
  • Warm up by driving gently, not by idling. Keep revs moderate until the oil is warm.
  • Let the turbo settle. After hard motorway driving, drive the last minute or two gently before switching off.
  • Refill AdBlue early. When the range reaches zero the car will not restart.
  • Wrong fuel. If petrol goes into the tank, do not start the engine. Call roadside assistance.
